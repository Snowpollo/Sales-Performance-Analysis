# Sales Performance Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes 2,000 sales transactions to evaluate overall sales performance and identify trends across products, regions, customer types, sales channels, and salespeople.

The analysis was completed using Microsoft Excel, with Pivot Tables and visualizations used to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project was to understand the company's sales performance and identify the areas contributing most to revenue.

The analysis focused on:

- Overall revenue performance
- Product and product category performance
- Regional sales performance
- Sales channel performance
- New vs. returning customer revenue
- Monthly sales trends
- Salesperson performance

## 🔍 Key Analysis & Findings

### Product Category Performance

The Computers category was the strongest-performing category, generating approximately $1.35 million in revenue and accounting for more than half of total revenue.

Monitors were the second-largest category, generating approximately $526,745.

### Product Performance

The highest-revenue products included:

| Product | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Gaming Laptop | $432,576 |
| Laptop Air 13 | $273,533 |
| Laptop Pro 14 | $263,486 |
| All-in-One PC | $254,994 |
| Ultrawide Monitor | $190,959 |

The Gaming Laptop was the highest-revenue individual product.

### Regional Performance

| Region | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| North | $561,754 |
| Central | $556,705 |
| East | $467,809 |
| South | $444,494 |
| West | $421,322 |

The North generated the highest regional revenue, while the West generated the lowest.

### Sales Channel Performance

| Sales Channel | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Online | $791,537 |
| Corporate Sales | $718,745 |
| Retail Store | $631,399 |
| Marketplace | $310,403 |

Online sales generated the highest revenue among the four sales channels.

### Customer Type Analysis

Returning customers generated approximately $1.68 million in revenue compared with approximately $768,759 from new customers.

This indicates that returning customers were a major contributor to overall revenue.

### Monthly Sales Performance

November recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $274,368, while July recorded the lowest at approximately $150,713.

This variation suggests that seasonal demand, promotions, or other factors may influence monthly sales performance.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

- Prioritize high-performing products such as Gaming Laptops and other computer products.
- Strengthen customer retention strategies because returning customers contribute significantly to revenue.
- Investigate the performance gap between the strongest and weakest regions.
- Evaluate why Marketplace sales are considerably lower than other channels.
- Investigate seasonal sales patterns to improve sales planning and forecasting.
